Endpoints

One entry is a provider: OpenAI itself, or another OpenAI-compatible endpoint at its base URL, with the key it takes and the models it serves. Keys are stored in webui/models.json, readable by its owner alone and kept out of git. A key is never shown again, never sent back to this page, and never put on a command line — it reaches the subject through its environment.

Thinking

How each model is asked to reason, chosen here and nowhere else: the Episode and Campaign pages show it next to the model, and every run records it. model default sends nothing. Each family is asked the way the subjects ask it (sut/common/thinking.py): the GPT, o-series, Claude and Gemini families take an effort level, minimal to high; Qwen takes a chat-template switch and no effort level; Gemma and Ministral ship reasoning as a separate checkpoint and cannot be turned on. A setting the family refuses is refused when the endpoint is saved.
