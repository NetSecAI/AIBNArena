A matrix of episodes: subjects × models × scenarios × seeds, run one at a time against as few subject processes as it can. Records, reports and logs are written under reports/campaigns/runs/<campaign>/, which is where scripts/export_results.py is pointed afterwards.

Scenarios

One row is one fault of the matrix, run against every subject and model chosen above, and chosen the way the Episode page chooses one: the scenario, the wording of its task, its method, then the topology and the reference state it runs from. Tick false positive to run it on a healthy lab with no fault injected, where any attempted mutation is the failure. Each row carries its own seed, drawn at launch and written down with the fault it resolved to, so the same scenario added twice is two different faults — which is how a scenario is measured on more than one.

Advanced applied to every cell

Only settings every subject accepts are offered here. A matrix that refused its third subject over a flag the first two took would have wasted the two runs before it.
